# Questions people ask

The home page shows these, search engines read them as an FAQ, and AI assistants
read them in llms.txt. Each question starts with "## " and its answer follows as
plain sentences. Keep the answers short, factual and true.

## What is Building Scout?
Building Scout is a free book by Christopher Haworth: a plain-English guide to AI agents, and the tools that build them. Over fifteen chapters you build Scout, a working AI research assistant, and put it live on the internet.

## Is the book really free?
Yes. The whole book is free to download as a PDF and an EPUB. You give your first name, surname and email address on buildingscout.info and get the download links straight away. You're only emailed about new chapters and books if you tick the box.

## Do I need to know how to code?
No. The book is written for complete beginners. If you can install an app, copy and paste, and follow a recipe, you can finish it. You build Scout by describing what you want to Claude Code, and every technical word is explained the first time it appears.

## What is Scout?
Scout is the AI research assistant you build in the book. You ask it a question; a planner agent splits it into tasks, researcher agents search the web in parallel, a writer turns their findings into a report where every claim cites its source, and a fact-checker reviews it. It's built with Python, FastAPI, LangGraph and the Claude API.

## What will I learn?
What an AI agent actually is, how to work with Claude Code, how to write good prompts, and how to give agents tools. You also learn hooks, subagents, skills, MCP (the Model Context Protocol), LangGraph workflows, long-running harnesses and agents that improve themselves in a loop.

## What do I need to get started?
A computer running macOS, Windows 10 or 11, or Linux; a Claude subscription (Pro or Max) or an Anthropic Console account; a little API credit, since each research question costs a few pence and ten pounds is plenty for the whole book; and a free GitHub account.

## Can I see Scout working before I start?
Yes. The demo at buildingscout.info/demo replays real Scout runs step by step: the plan, the researchers' web searches, the cited report and the fact-check.

## Who wrote it?
Christopher (Chris) Haworth, a product leader in SaaS and digital communications who specialises in personalisation and optimisation. He isn't a developer: he started out dabbling in front-end work (HTML, CSS, PHP and JavaScript), and now builds projects with Claude to explore what AI can do.
